------------
--- Gets ---
------------
Used to interact with the user terminal, getting the information written on it.
ex.:
     puts "Hello, what's your name?"
     name = gets
     puts "Your name is #{name}"

terminal: 
     Hello, what's your name?
user:
     John
terminal
     Your name is John

To avoid getting new lines after the gets use ".chomp" at the end of you gets.
