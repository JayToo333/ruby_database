-------------
--- PRINT ---
-------------
There are multiple ways to print something in Ruby:

Print - Print the text between quotation marks or variables without jumping any lines after. 
ex.:
      print "Hello World"

terminal:
      Hello World

ex2.: 
      print "Hello World"
      print "Hello World"

terminal:
      Hello WorldHello World 

Puts - Same thing as Print, however it creates a new line for the next print (like what enter does on a notepad)
ex.: 
      puts "Hello World"
      puts "Hello World"

terminal:
      Hello World
      Hello World
